        Grachtengoud

        Ten caches of canal gold lie buried near Amsterdam’s landmarks. Solve the riddles in your logbook, follow the detector, and dig them all up.

        • WalkWASD or arrows. The detector shows signal strength within 300 m, but no direction.
        • RunHold Shift. Fast, but the detector is off and stamina drains.
        • Bend overC. Slow, but within 90 m you get the exact distance and an arrow.
        • DigSpace or E while bent over, within 12 m of a cache.

        V camera · M city map · L logbook. Coins on the streets and quick finds in a row raise your score. On a phone, use the joystick and the round buttons.

        Multiplayer race

        Everyone hunts the same ten caches. Each one goes to whoever digs it up first.
